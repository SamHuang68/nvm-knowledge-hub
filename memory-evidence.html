EVIDENCE METHOD

Claim → Evidence → Inference，
不跨級偷換。Claim → Evidence → Inference,
without category errors.

技術問題以論文、專利與原廠文件為主。專利用於證明 sensing topology 的存在；供應商文件用於描述自家 architecture 與測試主張；攻擊難度與普遍性只由第三方實證支撐。Technical claims rely on papers, patents and first-party documentation. Patents establish that a sensing topology exists; vendor documents describe architecture and internal test claims; only independent demonstrations support attack feasibility and generality.
