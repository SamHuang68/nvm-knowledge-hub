LENS 01 · WAFER SIGN-OFF RIGOR

晶圓認證三道門禁：邁向 AEC-Q100 Grade 0 零缺陷 Wafer Qualification 3-Gate Rigor: Achieving AEC-Q100 Grade 0

車規晶圓驗證必須通過業界最嚴苛的跨批次試驗與早期失效率篩選，透過物理層微結構擊穿控制，實現出廠零缺陷 (<0.001 PPM) 的可靠度目標。 Automotive wafer qualification demands the industry's strictest multi-lot audits and infant mortality screening, ensuring zero-defect (<0.001 PPM) operational reliability.

GATE 01 · WAFER LOT AUDIT

3-Lot 跨批次高溫壽命試驗 (HTOL) 3-Lot Cross-Lot HTOL

跨越 3 個非連續晶圓生產批次，每批抽取 77 顆晶片在 175°C 結溫與 1.1× 標稱電壓下持續運行 1,000 小時。零單元擊穿退化，拒絕任何軟性位元翻轉。 Across three non-consecutive wafer lots, 77 dice per lot run 1,000 hours at 175°C Tj and 1.1× nominal voltage. Zero cell breakdown; no soft-bit flips accepted.

GATE 02 · STRESS CYCLING

-55°C ~ 175°C 極限熱衝擊與 ELFR -55°C ~ 175°C Thermal Shock & ELFR

承受 1,000 次極限溫差循環熱衝擊，考核封裝膨脹剪切應力。搭配 48 小時早期失效率 (ELFR) 高溫篩選，消除浴盆曲線初期微觀缺陷。 1,000 extreme thermal-shock cycles to qualify package expansion shear, paired with a 48-hour ELFR high-temperature screen to remove bathtub-curve infant defects.

GATE 03 · PACKAGING INTEGRITY

迴焊熱耐受與金凸塊剪切力驗證 Reflow Endurance & Au-Bump Shear

通過 260°C 無鉛迴焊爐峰值衝擊。金屬矽化物共晶溫度高達 >850°C，徹底免疫高溫退火，確保在引擎周邊與逆變器長效運作 15 年以上。 Survives 260°C Pb-free reflow peak. Silicide eutectic above 850°C is immune to high-temperature anneal, supporting 15+ years beside engine and inverter.
