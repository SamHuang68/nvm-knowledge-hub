LENS 01 · WAFER CHECKLIST RIGOR

晶圓認證三道門禁：邁向 AEC-Q100 Grade 0 低缺陷任務載記 Wafer Qualification 3-Gate Rigor: Achieving AEC-Q100 Grade 0

車規晶圓驗證需跨批次試驗與早期失效率篩選；本頁以任務載記與檢核清單語彙說明低缺陷目標，非宣稱已達 Zero-PPM 或 <0.001 PPM 認證。 Automotive wafer qualification involves multi-lot audits and infant-mortality screening; this page uses mission-profile checklist vocabulary for low-defectivity targets — not a claim of Zero-PPM or <0.001 PPM certification.

GATE 01 · WAFER LOT AUDIT

3-Lot 跨批次高溫壽命試驗 (HTOL) 3-Lot Cross-Lot HTOL

跨越 3 個非連續晶圓生產批次，每批抽取 77 顆晶片在 175°C 結溫與 1.1× 標稱電壓下持續運行 1,000 小時。零單元擊穿退化，拒絕任何軟性位元翻轉。 Across three non-consecutive wafer lots, 77 dice per lot run 1,000 hours at 175°C Tj and 1.1× nominal voltage. Zero cell breakdown; no soft-bit flips accepted.

GATE 02 · STRESS CYCLING

-55°C ~ 150°C 極限熱衝擊與 ELFR -55°C ~ 150°C Thermal Shock & ELFR

承受 1,000 次極限溫差循環熱衝擊 (-55°C 至 +150°C)，考核封裝膨脹剪切應力。搭配 3 批次共 2,400 顆早期失效率 (ELFR, c=0) 高溫篩選，消除浴盆曲線初期微觀缺陷。 1,000 extreme thermal-shock cycles (-55°C to +150°C) to qualify package expansion shear, paired with 2,400-unit early life failure rate screening (ELFR, 3 lots, c=0) to eliminate infant mortality on the bathtub curve.

GATE 03 · PACKAGING INTEGRITY

迴焊熱耐受與高溫數據保持烘烤 Reflow Endurance & High-Temp Retention Bake

通過 260°C 無鉛迴焊爐峰值衝擊，並依據 AEC-Q100-005 / JESD22-A103 執行高溫數據保持烘烤 (150°C 1,000 小時或 175°C 加速烘烤)。永久閘極介電質擊穿形成局域再結晶矽導電通道，高溫退火下幾無電荷漏失退化，確保在引擎周邊與逆變器長效運作 15 年以上。 Survives 260°C Pb-free reflow peak, followed by AEC-Q100-005 / JESD22-A103 non-volatile data retention bake (150°C 1,000 hrs or equivalent 175°C accelerated bake). Permanent gate dielectric breakdown with localized recrystallized silicon percolation path exhibits named-condition ohmic stability (not absolute zero-drift certification) under thermal annealing, supporting architecture-class 15+ year automotive mission profiles.
