ISO 26262 與功能安全實作 ISO 26262 & Functional Safety Implementation

01

SECDED ECC (Single Error Correction, Double Error Detection)

內建錯誤修正碼，即時修復單元元翻轉 (Single bit-flip)，並偵測雙位元錯誤以發出中斷。 Built-in ECC instantly corrects single bit-flips and detects double bit errors to issue interrupts.

02

BIST 內建自我測試BIST (Built-In Self Test)

每次車輛啟動時自動檢查記憶體健康狀態，符合 ASIL 安全機制要求。 Automatically checks memory health status upon each vehicle start, complying with ASIL safety mechanism requirements.

03

安全存取控制Secure Access Control

硬體級鎖定與區域隔離，防止惡意篡改 ADAS/EV 關鍵校正資料。 Hardware-level locking and partitioning prevent malicious tampering of critical ADAS/EV calibration data.
