Single-bit upsets are corrected dynamically online; double-bit failures trigger hardware safety interrupts directly to the ECU.

① ADAS 攝影機光學畸變校準① ADAS Camera Distortion Calibration

車載鏡頭組裝時進行高精度光學畸變 (Lens Distortion) 與神經網絡權重微調。此參數不可丟失，否則將導致自駕系統對車道線距離誤判。AntiFuse 確保高溫暴曬下 15 年零遺失。 Camera modules store optical distortion tables and neural net bias trims. Losing this data causes autonomous driving distance miscalculations; AntiFuse guarantees permanent preservation.

② BMS 電池管理系統健康度 (SoH)② BMS Battery Health & Thermal Log

紀錄電池包的歷史充放電循環、健康度 (State of Health) 曲線與熱失控 (Thermal Runaway) 臨界閥值，作為電池保固理賠與車輛安全監控的法定不可竄改黑盒子。 Logs historical charge-discharge cycles, State of Health (SoH) degradation curves, and thermal runaway thresholds as a tamper-evident legal black box for battery warranties.

③ 驅動馬達逆變器 (Inverter) 死區補償③ Traction Inverter Dead-time Compensation

儲存 SiC / IGBT 功率開關之奈秒級死區時間 (Dead-time) 補償參數。若高溫下參數漂移，上下橋臂直通 (Shoot-through) 將導致逆變器瞬間燒毀，因此必須採用零漂移 AntiFuse 固化。 Stores nanosecond dead-time trim parameters for SiC/IGBT power bridges. Parameter drift risks bridge shoot-through short circuits; hence immutable AntiFuse storage is mandatory.
