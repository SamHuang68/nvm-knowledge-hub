從 XPU identity、HBM repair、CPO calibration 到 firmware 與 RAS evidence，本章先證明系統要求的行為，再標示 OTP、MTP、managed NVM 或 external storage 的架構推論。 From XPU identity and HBM repair to CPO calibration, firmware and RAS evidence, this chapter proves the required behavior first—then labels OTP, MTP, managed-NVM or external-storage choices as architecture inferences.
