Executive Speaker Notes (EN)

TSMC's 22ULL, 12FFC+, and N4e continuum offers sub-0.6V near-threshold operation, slashing dynamic power by over 75%. Our qualification thesis establishes pure-logic AntiFuse OTP as the primary on-chip MCU code storage macro. By leveraging hardware indirection jump tables, designers achieve virtual bug-patching without rewritable cells, delivering zero standby leakage and zero adder-mask cost across TSMC's leading-edge ultra-low-power logic nodes.

Our qualification contract embeds a dedicated, ultra-compact AntiFuse OTP and SRAM PUF Root of Trust into every individual chiplet, providing die-unique immutable IDs and pre-bond wafer probe keys that guarantee zero-thermal-penalty, zero-at-rest 3D packaging integrity.

NIST SP 800-208 狀態化啟動：耐受 175°C 金屬導電細絲物理機制NIST SP 800-208 Stateful Boot: 175°C Immune Metallic Filament Physics

08 · QUALIFY NOW · PQC & AUTOMOTIVE SILC

關鍵架構要點Architecture Highlights

  • 抗量子簽章 (LMS/XMSS) 僅需在 OTP 內保存極精簡 32 位元組根雜湊Post-quantum stateful signatures (LMS/XMSS) anchor to a compact 32-byte root hash
  • 一次性金鑰狀態 (OTS) 嚴禁重用，依賴硬體級不可逆單調遞增計數器One-time signing states strictly forbid reuse, requiring irreversible hardware counters
  • 車規 175°C AEC-Q100 Grade 0 結溫環境下傳統浮閘記憶體電荷嚴重流失Legacy floating-gate cells suffer severe charge leakage at 175°C automotive Grade 0
  • 奈米金屬導電細絲物理機制高溫零電荷漂移，奠定 ASIL-D 終極防禦Nanoscale metallic filament physics exhibit zero thermal charge drift, securing ASIL-D

講者備忘與戰略論述 (ZH-TW)

後量子密碼學 (PQC) 時代的降臨使傳統安全開機面臨巨大挑戰：基於晶格 (Lattice) 的非對稱演算法會造成公鑰體積暴增超過 40 倍，極大超出嵌入式晶片的儲存預算。NIST SP 800-208 推薦的狀態化雜湊簽章 (Stateful Hash-Based Signature, 如 LMS 與 XMSS)，僅需在晶片 OTP 中儲存極為精簡的 32 位元組根雜湊 (Root Hash)。然而，狀態化簽章的致命死穴在於一次性金鑰 (OTS) 絕對不可重複使用，否則密鑰系統瞬間瓦解。同時，車用動力總成 (Powertrain) 要求達到嚴苛的 AEC-Q100 Grade 0 (175°C 結溫) 可靠度，傳統浮閘 (Floating Gate) 記憶體在該高溫下會產生嚴重的應力誘發漏電 (SILC) 與電荷流失。Synopsys AntiFuse OTP 具備『奈米金屬矽化物永久熔斷導電細絲』的固體物理優勢，高溫零電荷漂移，且不可逆的物理燒斷特性保證一次性簽章單調計數器無法被竄改倒退，是抵禦量子威脅與車規極限環境的終極硬體根基。

Executive Speaker Notes (EN)

The post-quantum cryptography transition exposes conventional secure boot to acute silicon overhead: lattice-based signatures inflate key payloads by over 40x, overwhelming embedded non-volatile memory budgets. NIST SP 800-208 stateful hash-based signatures (LMS and XMSS) solve this by anchoring security to a compact 32-byte root hash in OTP. However, stateful schemes introduce a catastrophic vulnerability: one-time signature (OTS) leaf reuse completely breaks the private key. Concurrently, automotive powertrain applications mandate AEC-Q100 Grade 0 reliability at 175°C junction temperature, where legacy floating-gate cells suffer severe Stress-Induced Leakage Current (SILC) and trap-assisted discharge. Our AntiFuse OTP leverages irreversible nanoscale metallic silicide filament physics that exhibit zero thermal charge drift at 175°C, providing the physical hardware foundation for stateful anti-rollback protection and ASIL-D automotive compliance.
