PART 01 · POWER SEMICONDUCTORS

BCD 製程電源晶片：低密度精準微調與高密度協定控制 BCD Power Management: Low-Density Trimming to High-Density Protocol Control

Bipolar-CMOS-DMOS (BCD) 技術融合了精準類比感測 (Bipolar)、數位控制邏輯 (CMOS) 與高壓大電流開關 (DMOS)。傳統 eFlash 在 BCD 製程需額外 10~15 道光罩與高溫製程，重創毛利；原生 AntiFuse OTP / NeoMTP 達成零額外光罩 (Zero Mask Adder)，為次世代智慧電源晶片提供不可或缺的記憶體基石。 Bipolar-CMOS-DMOS (BCD) combines precision analog (Bipolar), digital logic (CMOS), and power switches (DMOS). While legacy eFlash requires 10-15 adder masks and destructive thermal budgets, native AntiFuse OTP and NeoMTP integrate with zero extra masks, serving as the critical enabler for smart power ICs.

PMIC 基準電壓與振盪器校準 PMIC Voltage Reference & Oscillator Calibration

半導體製造容差會導致帶隙基準源 (Bandgap Reference Vref) 產生 ±3% 至 ±5% 的偏差。透過在晶圓測試 (CP) 與最終測試 (FT) 階段燒錄 OTP 微調碼，可即時調整梯形電阻網 (Resistor Ladder) 與電流源，將輸出電壓精度提升至 ±0.5% 以內，並校準內部振盪器頻率 (Oscillator Frequency)，確保電源轉換器之開關頻率維持在預定 EMI 安全視窗內。 Silicon process variations induce ±3% to ±5% shifts in bandgap reference voltages (Vref). Burning OTP trim bits during Chip Probing (CP) and Final Test (FT) dynamically adjusts resistor ladders and current mirrors, tightening output accuracy within ±0.5% and stabilizing internal clock oscillators to keep switching frequencies within EMI safety windows.

  • 典型容量Typical Density: 128-bit to 2K-bit OTP / MTP
  • 關鍵參數Key Parameters: Vref trim, Over-Current Protection (OCP), Over-Voltage Protection (OVP), Thermal Shutdown threshold
  • 製程節點Process Nodes: 0.18µm, 0.13µm, 90nm, 55nm BCD

多通道 LED 驅動器恆流匹配 Multi-Channel LED Driver Current Balancing

車用頭燈矩陣、電視 Mini-LED 分區背光 (Local Dimming) 要求各通道間的恆流輸出誤差小於 1.5%。每一路驅動單元在封裝應力下會產生跨通道電阻不匹配，透過內嵌 OTP/MTP 儲存各通道獨立的數位增益補償碼 (Gain Compensation)，消除電流失配，避免顯示背光出現條紋或車燈亮度不均。 Automotive matrix headlights and Mini-LED local dimming displays demand channel-to-channel current matching tighter than 1.5%. Package-induced thermo-mechanical stress creates per-channel resistor disparities; on-chip OTP/MTP stores per-channel digital gain trims to eliminate luminance banding and hotspot artifacts.

  • 恆流精度Current Accuracy: Channel-to-channel mismatch <1.0% after electrical trim
  • 資料留存Retention: 15+ years @ 125°C to 150°C junction temperature
  • 經濟效益Economics: Eliminates external precision trimming resistors and laser trimming steps

USB-PD 3.1 · BLDC MOTOR FOC · AUTOMOTIVE BMIC

USB-PD 3.1 & 私有快充協議晶片 USB Power Delivery 3.1 & Fast-Charging Protocols

現代 USB Type-C 電源適配器需支援高達 240W (48V/5A Extended Power Range, EPR) 以及複雜的動態協商協議（PD 3.1、PPS 可程式電源供應、QC 5.0 等）。內嵌高密度 MTP 作為微控制器的程式碼與配置庫，支援開箱即用的供電配置檔 (PDO/APDO) 解析、電子標籤 (E-Marker) 密鑰認證，並允許出廠後因應協議修訂進行現場韌體更新 (In-System Update)。 Modern USB Type-C adapters support up to 240W (48V/5A EPR) and dynamic negotiation protocols (PD 3.1, PPS, QC 5.0). Embedded high-density MTP stores MCU firmware, Power Delivery Objects (PDOs), and cryptographic E-Marker attestation keys, enabling field firmware upgrades for evolving fast-charge standards.

BLDC 無刷直流馬達控制器 (MCU Code) BLDC Motor Drivers & Embedded MCU Code

無人機、伺服機器人、車用水泵與散熱風扇廣泛採用整合型 SoC 馬達驅動器。晶片內部整合 32-bit MCU（如 Arm Cortex-M0+ 或 RISC-V）以執行磁場導向向量控制 (FOC)、空間向量脈寬調變 (SVPWM) 與無感測器 (Sensorless) 轉子角度估算。高密度 eNVM 提供零等待 (Zero-Wait State) 的指令讀取，免去昂貴外部 SPI Flash，節省 PCB 面積並杜絕干擾。 Drones, robotics, automotive pumps and fans rely on integrated SoC motor drivers. These chips house a 32-bit MCU (Arm Cortex-M0+ / RISC-V) running Field Oriented Control (FOC) and sensorless rotor observers. High-density eNVM enables zero-wait execution without external SPI Flash, drastically shrinking BOM footprint.

DENSITY: 32KB - 128KB MTP/OTP EXECUTION: ZERO-WAIT CODE READ

BMIC 電池管理晶片 & 類比前端 (AFE) Battery Management ICs & Analog Frontend (AFE)

在電動車 (EV) 與儲能系統 (ESS) 中，BMIC 需同時監控 16 至 24 節鋰電池單元的電壓、溫度與充放電狀態。內嵌 eNVM 用於儲存電池化學開路電壓 (OCV-SOC) 查找表、庫倫計增益漂移係數、高溫老化補償模型，以及通過 ISO 26262 ASIL-D 嚴苛車規認證之安全臨界值與故障日誌黑盒子。 In EVs and energy storage, BMICs monitor 16-24 series cell voltages and temperatures. Embedded eNVM stores chemistry Open Circuit Voltage (OCV-SOC) look-up tables, coulomb counter calibration constants, cell aging models, and ISO 26262 ASIL-D fault logging registers.

PART 02 · MATRIX YIELD & DEFECT REPAIR

CIS 與 DRAM 矩陣修復：高密集度製程缺陷的重映射架構 CIS & DRAM Array Repair: Remapping Architecture for Dense Matrix Defects

在高解析度 CMOS 影像感測器 (CIS) 與先進 DRAM / HBM 中，百萬乃至數十億個微型單元具備極高的物理缺陷機率。若無修復機制，單一缺陷點將導致整顆晶片報廢。內建自我測試 (BIST)、備援分析 (BIRA) 與 AntiFuse OTP 構成現代晶片良率修復黃金三角。 In ultra-dense CMOS Image Sensors (CIS) and cutting-edge DRAM / HBM, millions to billions of sub-micron cells face inevitable manufacturing defects. Without autonomous repair, a single faulted bitcell destroys multi-hundred-dollar dies. BIST, BIRA, and AntiFuse OTP establish the core triad for high-yield volume manufacturing.

01

BIST

Built-In Self-Test

晶圓探針或上電自檢發動 March C- 演算法與棋盤格掃描，精確定位開路、短路、漏電及耦合缺陷位址。 Executes March C- and checkerboard test patterns to pinpoint open, short, leakage, and coupling faults across dense bitcell arrays.

➔
02

BIRA

Built-In Redundancy Analysis

硬體演算法以有限的備援行 (Spare Rows) 與備援列 (Spare Columns)，計算出最佳的覆蓋解，最大化晶粒良率。 Hardware solver calculates the mathematical optimum to cover detected faults using allocated spare rows and spare columns.

➔
03

FuseBox OTP

Non-Volatile Remapping

將修復映射表 (Repair Signature) 永久寫入 AntiFuse OTP；每次開機即時重定向解碼器，透明隔離壞死單元。 Permanently burns repair signatures into on-die AntiFuse OTP, transparently redirecting memory decoders upon every boot.

1. CMOS 影像感測器 (CIS) 壞點修復與 3D 堆疊 1. CMOS Image Sensor (CIS) Pixel Repair & 3D Stacking

在 48MP、108MP 乃至 2 億畫素的手機與車用 CIS 中，微米級光電二極體 (Photodiode) 與傳輸閘極 (TX) 容易因矽晶格缺陷或重金屬污染產生熱像素 (Hot Pixels) 或死像素 (Dead Pixels)。現代 3 堆疊 CIS（Pixel Array + Logic ISP + DRAM Buffer）透過底部邏輯層內嵌的 AntiFuse OTP 儲存高達 16K~64K-bit 的壞點座標與增益校正係數，由硬體 ISP 進行即時周邊像素中值插補 (Bilinear Interpolation)，實現 100% 視覺無暇輸出。 In 48MP to 200MP mobile and automotive CIS, micron-scale photodiodes develop hot/dead pixels due to silicon lattice dislocations or metal impurities. Modern 3-layer stacked CIS (Pixel Array + ISP Logic + DRAM Cache) utilizes AntiFuse OTP in the logic die to store 16K-64K bits of defective pixel coordinates, enabling real-time bilinear interpolation and artifact-free imaging.

  • 修復型態Repair Mode: Dead pixel table remap + Lens shading compensation + Dark current trim
  • 先進封裝Packaging: TSMC 3DFabric, Wafer-to-Wafer (W2W) hybrid bonding

2. DRAM / HBM3e 封裝後現場修復 (Post-Package Repair, PPR) 2. DRAM / HBM3e Post-Package Repair (PPR)

JEDEC DDR5 與 HBM3/3e 標準強制規範了硬體級「封裝後修復 (PPR)」架構。傳統雷射熔斷 (Laser Fuse) 只能在封裝前晶圓階段進行；而 AntiFuse OTP 具備微小面積與低燒錄功耗優勢，允許伺服器在資料中心運行過程中，偵測到不可更正記憶體錯誤 (Uncorrectable Error / 2-bit ECC Failure) 時，發動 hPPR (Hard PPR) 指令，將備援記憶體 Row 的重新映射永久寫入晶粒內部的 AntiFuse FuseBox，挽救價值上萬美元的高階 AI 伺服器模組。 JEDEC DDR5 and HBM3/3e mandate Post-Package Repair (PPR). While obsolete laser fuses are strictly restricted to un-packaged wafers, AntiFuse OTP enables in-system repair. When hyperscale datacenters encounter uncorrectable multi-bit ECC errors, runtime hPPR commands permanently burn spare-row remap vectors into the on-die FuseBox, averting catastrophic server crashes.

  • 標準規格Standard: JEDEC JESD79-5 DDR5 & JESD238 HBM3e hPPR / sPPR compliant
  • 可靠度優勢Reliability: Zero post-burn conductivity rebound, 100% immune to laser targeting errors

修復技術演進歷程：Laser Fuse ➔ eFuse ➔ AntiFuse OTP Evolution of Repair Storage: Laser Fuse ➔ Poly eFuse ➔ AntiFuse OTP

LEGACY

Laser Fuse (雷射熔斷)

使用實體雷射燒斷金屬線。缺點：佔用巨大開窗面積，無法在晶片封裝後修復，雷射定位精度極限無法微縮至 28nm 以下。Mechanically vaporizes metal links with lasers. High pad overhead, zero post-package repair capability, extinct below 28nm.

TRANSITIONAL

eFuse (電遷移電熔絲)

施加大電流透過電遷移熔斷多晶矽/金屬。缺點：初始為低阻導通，燒斷後易有金屬回彈造成漏電；且每個 bit 需要巨大大電流驅動晶體，容量難以超越 4K-bit。Blows polysilicon via high-current electromigration. Prone to conductive regrowth; huge driving transistors restrict density below 4K-bit.

MODERN STANDARD

AntiFuse OTP (反熔絲氧化層崩潰)

初始為絕緣高阻態，燒錄時在高壓下擊穿閘極介電質形成永久金屬矽化物微絲。面積僅 eFuse 1/5，容量可達 128Kb，支援晶圓級與封裝後全生命週期自我修復。Starts in high-impedance state; dielectric breakdown forms permanent metallic filament. 1/5th area of eFuse, scalable to 128Kb, enables lifetime PPR.

PART 03 · DISPLAY DRIVERS & OPTOELECTRONICS

高壓顯示驅動 (HV DDIC)：Gamma 校準、Vcom 防閃爍與 De-Mura 補償 High-Voltage Display Drivers (HV DDIC): Gamma Calibration, Vcom Tuning & De-Mura

面板驅動晶片 (DDIC) 面臨極為嚴峻的高壓電氣環境（Gate 高達 +20V/-15V，Source 達 8V~12V）。高階 AMOLED、LTPO 智慧手機面板與 Micro-LED 顯示器對色彩一致性與刷新均勻度有極高要求。內嵌 High-Voltage eNVM 是實現出廠光學校準與像素級均勻度消除的致勝關鍵。 Display Driver ICs (DDICs) operate in severe high-voltage domains (Gate rails up to +20V/-15V, Source lines up to 8V-12V). Premium AMOLED, LTPO mobile screens, and Micro-LED panels demand absolute color consistency and refresh uniformity, powered by on-chip High-Voltage eNVM.

1. Gamma 2.2 曲線多階校準 1. Multi-Point Gamma 2.2 Curve Calibration

人眼對亮度的感知呈非線性對數響應（典型標準為 γ = 2.2）。然而每塊面板的薄膜電晶體 (TFT) 遷移率存在先天材料離散性。DDIC 內嵌 OTP/MTP 儲存紅、綠、藍 (RGB) 獨立的可程式化數位類比轉換器 (R-DAC) 基準電壓點（如 64 階至 256 階微調值），精確擬合理想色彩灰階曲線，杜絕低灰階色彩失真與白平衡偏移。 Human luminance perception follows a non-linear power curve (γ = 2.2). Thin-Film Transistor (TFT) mobility variations introduce per-panel color skews. Embedded OTP/MTP stores independent R/G/B resistor-string DAC reference registers (64 to 256 voltage points), mapping panel luminance to precise color standards and eliminating low-grayscale chromatic aberration.

2. Vcom 電壓精準微調 (防閃爍) 2. Vcom Anti-Flicker Precision Voltage Tuning

在液晶 (LCD) 面板中，若像素電極兩端存在長期直流分量 (DC Bias)，會導致液晶分子離子化造成永久殘影 (Image Sticking)。因此必須施加極性反轉交流驅動。公共電極電壓 (Vcom) 必須精確調節至正負擺幅的絕對幾何中點；哪怕僅有 5mV 至 10mV 的微小偏差，就會引發人眼可察覺的 30Hz/60Hz 畫面閃爍 (Flicker)。OTP/MTP 永久寫入最佳微調設定。 In LCD panels, sustained DC bias across pixel electrodes causes liquid crystal degradation and permanent image sticking. Driver ICs alternate polarity via AC drive. The common electrode voltage (Vcom) must align perfectly with the electrical midpoint; an offset as tiny as 5-10mV produces noticeable 30Hz/60Hz display flicker. Embedded OTP locks in the factory-tuned Vcom optimum.

3. OLED De-Mura 光學均勻性補償 3. OLED De-Mura Optical Compensation

OLED 顯示器在微觀蒸鍍過程中，有機發光層厚度與驅動 TFT 閾值電壓微小偏差會產生肉眼可見的發光不均勻暗斑（稱為 Mura）。高階 DDIC 採用 De-Mura 技術：在產線以高精度 CCD 相機拍攝螢幕各區域發光特性，計算出像素級亮度增益修正矩陣 (De-Mura LUT) 並儲存於 eNVM，即時調制像素驅動電流，達成渾然一體的均勻顯示。 In AMOLED manufacturing, subtle organic deposition variations and TFT Vth mismatches create luminance non-uniformity clouds known as Mura. High-end DDICs incorporate De-Mura hardware: high-resolution cameras capture panel luminance anomalies, compute a pixel-level gain correction look-up table (LUT) stored in eNVM, and dynamically equalize sub-pixel drive currents.
