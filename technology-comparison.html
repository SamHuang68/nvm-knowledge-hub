03 · FIRST-PRINCIPLES MODELS

第一性原理物理模型與晶圓成本經濟學方程式 First-Principles Physics & Wafer Economics Equations

三條可檢查的恆等式：穿隧崩潰、光罩加價、次閾值漏電。用來約束選型，不是代工廠報價，也不是出貨證明。 Three inspectable identities — breakdown tunneling, mask-adder cost, subthreshold leakage — constrain selection. They are not foundry quotes and not shipment proof.

EQUATION 01 · ANTIFUSE BREAKDOWN

Fowler–Nordheim 穿隧與崩潰電流 Fowler–Nordheim tunneling current

薄閘極氧化層在高電場（Eox > 10 MV/cm）下誘發載子穿隧與撞擊游離，形成局域導電微絲： Carrier tunneling and percolation under extreme oxide field (Eox > 10 MV/cm) form a local conductive filament:

JFN = AFN · Eox2 · exp(−BFN / Eox)
• AFN = q3 / (8π h ΦB)，BFN = 8π √(2m*) ΦB3/2 / (3 q h)
• ΦB ≈ 3.15 eV（Si–SiO2 界面能障），m* ≈ 0.5 m0
• 編程後模型：阻值由 >1011 Ω 降至約 102 Ω。這是位元胞物理描述，不是出貨規格。 • AFN = q3 / (8π h ΦB), BFN = 8π √(2m*) ΦB3/2 / (3 q h)
• ΦB ≈ 3.15 eV (Si–SiO2 barrier), m* ≈ 0.5 m0
• Programmed-state model: resistance falls from >1011 Ω to ~102 Ω. Physics description, not a shipment spec.
EQUATION 02 · WAFER COST ADDER

額外光罩晶圓成本恆等式 Mask-adder wafer cost identity

用額外光罩道數 Nextra 與良率折損 ΔYnvm，把製程加價寫成可檢查的成本恆等式，而不是代工廠報價： Express process adder as an inspectable identity in extra masks Nextra and yield hit ΔYnvm — not a foundry quote:

Cwafer = Cbase · (1 + Σ γi) / (Ybase − ΔYnvm)
• eFlash：公開文獻常見 Nextra = 8–12，ΔC 量級約 +30%–45%
• STT-MRAM：BEOL 常見 Nextra = 3–5，ΔC 量級約 +15%–22%
• AntiFuse OTP：本恆等式取 Nextra = 0（邏輯相容）。零額外光罩 ≠ 晶圓免費。 • eFlash: public literature often cites Nextra = 8–12; ΔC order +30%–45%
• STT-MRAM: BEOL often Nextra = 3–5; ΔC order +15%–22%
• AntiFuse OTP: this identity sets Nextra = 0 (logic-compatible). Zero extra masks ≠ free wafers.
EQUATION 03 · STATIC LEAKAGE

次閾值漏電與長效待機模型 Subthreshold leakage, always-on domain

超低功耗邊緣節點 Always-On 域，靜態漏電由次閾值熱游離擴散主導： Static leakage in always-on edge MCU domains is dominated by subthreshold diffusion:

Ileak = I0 · 10(Vgs − Vth)/S · (1 − exp(−q Vds / kBT))
• 亞閾值擺幅 S ≈ 70–80 mV/dec @ 300 K
• 感測電路若可 power-gate，靜態漏電可壓到 <10 nA 量級。數字是設計目標，需矽驗證。 • Subthreshold swing S ≈ 70–80 mV/dec @ 300 K
• If the sense path can be power-gated, static leakage can reach <10 nA class. Target, not silicon proof by itself.
04 · FOUNDRY & SECURITY

四大晶圓代工廠製程路線與奈米實體防護機制 Foundry Ecosystem Roadmap & Physical Security Integrity

TAIWAN SEMICONDUCTOR

TSMC

  • 22ULL: 0.6V–0.8V 超低漏電；邏輯 OTP 作為 28nm eFlash 的 0-mask 替代路徑。0.6–0.8V ultra-low leakage; logic OTP as a 0-mask path versus 28nm eFlash.
  • 12FFC+ / N6e: FinFET，可到 0.5V 近閾值；不再依賴厚氧化層幫浦。FinFET, down to 0.5V near-threshold; thick-oxide pumps not required.
  • N4e / N2 GAA: 奈米片架構上的邏輯相容 OTP 驗證路徑。Nanosheet logic-compatible OTP qualification path.
UNITED MICROELECTRONICS

UMC

  • 28eHV / 40eHV: 30V–40V 顯示驅動與 OLED Gamma / De-Mura 微調。30–40V DDIC and OLED gamma / de-mura trim.
  • 22uLP / 22uLL: IoT 主力節點；避免浮閘熱預算進入 BCD 飄移區。IoT nodes; keep floating-gate thermal budget out of BCD drift regions.
GLOBALFOUNDRIES

GlobalFoundries

  • 22FDX (FD-SOI): 背閘極偏壓可用於編程電流調整；幅度以 PDK 為準。Back-bias can assist program current; magnitude is PDK-specific.
  • 12LP+ FinFET: 邊緣 AI 推論晶片的硬體信任根候選路徑。Candidate root-of-trust path for edge-AI inference dies.
SAMSUNG FOUNDRY

Samsung Foundry

  • 28FDS (Commercial eMRAM): 量產 BEOL MRAM；需面對 260°C 封裝迴焊熱退火。Production BEOL MRAM; 260°C package-reflow anneal remains a limit.
  • 8LPU / 4LPP: 行動處理器金鑰儲存與 PUF 整合路徑。Mobile key-storage and PUF integration path.
SILICON-LEVEL ANTI-TAMPER SECURITY

穿透式電子顯微鏡 (TEM) 與差分功耗分析 (DPA) 實體防禦機制 TEM Cross-Section Invisibility & DPA Side-Channel Immunity

① TEM / FIB visibility
AntiFuse 崩潰微絲約 3–8nm，藏在標準閘極氧化層中。對比 eFuse 的熔斷空洞與 Mask ROM 的 contact，破壞性逆向較難讀出。這是幾何論述，不是攻擊實驗結果。 AntiFuse filaments are ~3–8nm in standard gate oxide, harder to read than eFuse melt voids or Mask ROM contacts after de-layer. Geometry argument, not an attack-lab result.
② Complementary differential read (DPA)
互補位元對（A=HRS，B=LRS）同時抽取兩條位元線電流，瞬態功耗近似 Itotal = IHRS + ILRS，與資料的相關係數趨近於 0。這是側信道模型，不是攻擊證明。 A complementary pair (A=HRS, B=LRS) draws both bitlines so transient power tracks Itotal = IHRS + ILRS, and correlation with data approaches 0. Side-channel model, not an attack proof.
05 · ARCHITECT PIPELINE

三步收斂：架構師 NVM 決策簽核管線 Three-Stage Architectural NVM Selection Pipeline

STEP 01 GATE 1 · AUDIT

狀態契約與生命週期審查 State Contract Audit

區分不可變身分 (Immutable ID)、校準邊界 (Calibration) 與韌體代碼。若資料僅需工廠寫入或偶發修補，優先評估 AntiFuse + Patch CAM。 Distinguish immutable identity from firmware. For write-once or bounded patch models, evaluate AntiFuse paired with Vector CAM.

CRITERION: 0-Overhead Retention · Zero Bus Leak
STEP 02 GATE 2 · BUDGET

製程微縮與熱預算可行性 Process Node & Thermal Budget

檢查晶圓代工廠 PDK。若節點小於 28nm，eFlash 直接否決；評估是否具備 3~5 道 BEOL 光罩預算導入 MRAM，或採用 0-Mask 純邏輯相容方案。 Verify foundry PDK roadmap. If scaling beyond 28nm, rule out eFlash. Decide between BEOL mask adders (MRAM) and 0-mask logic NVM.

CRITERION: 0-Mask Logic Compatibility · ≤450°C Safe
STEP 03 GATE 3 · QUALIFIED

實體驗證與防禦簽核 Physical Verification Sign-Off

驗證 AEC-Q100 175°C 高溫工作壽命 (HTOL 1000h) 與 TEM 逆向防護標準，確保全生命週期不發生歐姆微絲電阻漂移。 Certify against AEC-Q100 175°C HTOL 1000h conditions and TEM cross-section anti-tamper standards to prevent filament resistance drift.

CRITERION: TEM Invisibility · Zero Drift @ 175°C
