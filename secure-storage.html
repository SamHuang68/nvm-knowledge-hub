THE EXECUTIVE THESIS

安全不只取決於資料能保存多久。
更取決於攻擊發生時，晶片裡還留下什麼。

OTP 解決永久性；Secure Storage 解決機密性。即使攻擊者成功觀察記憶體狀態，他取得的也應該是受保護資料，而不是可直接使用的 root secret。

PUBLIC ATTACK SIGNAL

用公開攻擊界定 threat model，
再把它轉成產品要求。

RP2350 是特定實作的公開 case，不代表所有 OTP 都同樣可攻。它揭示兩條平行路徑：fault 可能破壞 control assumptions；FIB／PVC 則接近 physical bit-state recovery。產品要求因此是：即使 readout 成功，也不應直接得到 usable secret。

01A · CONTROL PATH

Fault can collapse assumptions

01B · INVASIVE PATH

FIB/PVC can expose physical state

公開案例已讀出相鄰 bit pair 的 OR；完整逐 bit recovery 仍未被示範。

Encrypt, scramble and govern

LAYERED TRUST · PRACTICAL CASE STUDY

不是三選一。讓每一層各自解決正確的問題。Not three alternatives.Give each layer the right security job.

OpenPGP 保護資料與簽章；Okta FastPass 管理使用者、裝置狀態與存取政策；PUF 強化兩者下方的 silicon root，降低對永久儲存 root secret 的依賴。OpenPGP protects data and signatures. Okta FastPass governs users, device posture and access policy. A PUF strengthens the silicon root beneath them by reducing dependence on a persistently stored root secret.

01 · DATA

OpenPGP

主要任務Primary job端對端加密、解密與數位簽章。End-to-end encryption, decryption and digital signatures.

斷電後仍存在Persists at rest私鑰 packet；可能受 passphrase 保護，但仍是可保存與複製的數位物件。A secret-key packet, optionally passphrase-protected, remains a storable and copyable digital object.

不負責裝置完整性、登入政策或已解鎖後的惡意操作。Does not provide device integrity, login policy or protection from misuse after unlock.
02 · ACCESS

Okta FastPass

主要任務Primary jobphishing-resistant、passwordless authentication 與裝置政策。Phishing-resistant, passwordless authentication and device policy.

裝置端狀態Device state註冊時建立 proof-of-possession key；可使用 TPM／Secure Enclave，也可能落在 software keystore。Enrollment creates a proof-of-possession key; it may use a TPM/Secure Enclave or a software keystore.

hardware protection 必須由政策要求，不能只因為使用 FastPass 就假設存在。Hardware protection must be required by policy; FastPass alone does not guarantee it.
03 · SILICON ROOT

SRAM PUF

主要任務Primary job需要時重建 device-unique root，用來 derive 或 unwrap operational keys。Reconstruct a device-unique root on demand to derive or unwrap operational keys.

斷電後仍存在Persists at resthelper／activation data 與 wrapped keys 可以存在；root secret 本身不必永久儲存。Helper or activation data and wrapped keys may persist; the root secret itself need not.

不取代 user identity、MFA、authorization、revocation 或 recovery。Does not replace user identity, MFA, authorization, revocation or recovery.

主張邊界CLAIM BOUNDARY

PUF 的精確優勢不是「比 Okta 或 OpenPGP 更安全」，而是針對 powered-off physical extraction 與 device cloning，移除永久儲存 root-key target。最終結果仍取決於 reliability、helper-data integrity、FI／SCA、key residency、enrollment 與 recovery evidence。The precise PUF advantage is not “more secure than Okta or OpenPGP.” It removes a persistently stored root-key target for powered-off physical extraction and device cloning. The result still depends on reliability, helper-data integrity, FI/SCA, key residency, enrollment and recovery evidence.
