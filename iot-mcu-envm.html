在物聯網與超低功耗微控制器設計中，傳統 eFlash 長期面臨兩大工程瓶頸：一是傳統 NMOS 熱電子寫入電流大（mA 級）引發的電荷泵面積與熱應力；二是讀取電壓門檻多高於 1.8V，無法與 0.9V～1.2V 超低壓邏輯核心共軌。台灣 NVM IP 產業發展出兩條獨特的架構革新路徑： In IoT and ultra-low-power MCU designs, conventional eFlash faces two historic bottlenecks: high NMOS hot-electron programming currents (mA-class) burdening charge pumps, and a rigid ≥1.8V read voltage threshold conflicting with 0.9V–1.2V ultra-low-voltage logic cores. Taiwan's specialized NVM IP ecosystem addresses these via two distinct architectural paradigms:

常憶科技 Chingis Technology (chingistek.com) Chingis Technology (chingistek.com) pFusion™ / pFlash™

P-Channel 浮閘低功耗平台：獨創 P 型通道 PMOS 浮閘架構，以帶帶穿隧誘發熱電洞注入 (BBHH / BTBT) 實現極低電流編程（µA 級），大幅精簡片上電荷泵並降低對周圍邏輯元件的干擾；與標準 CMOS 邏輯製程高度相容，為智慧卡、低功耗 MCU 與 IoT 節點提供緊湊的 eFlash 嵌入式方案。 P-Channel Floating-Gate Platform: Pioneers P-Channel PMOS floating-gate architectures using band-to-band tunneling hot-hole injection (BBHH/BTBT) for microamp-class write currents, drastically downsizing charge pumps and suppressing logic disturbance while delivering CMOS-compatible eFlash for smart cards and IoT MCUs.

智憶科技 IOTMemory (iotmemory.com) IOTMemory Technology (iotmemory.com) SilvoFlash™ / SilvoFE™

0.9V～1.2V 原生超低壓 eFlash 突破：專利單層/低光罩 CMOS SilvoFlash™ 技術徹底破除傳統 eFlash ≥1.8V 讀取供電障礙，使整個 SoC 在近閾值核心電壓下直接原生讀取；推進 40nm 嵌入式 Flash SoC 合作，並實現 DDR5 SPD IC 與邊緣運算晶片量產，兼具代碼 eFlash 與資料 eEEPROM (SilvoFE™) 整合優勢。 0.9V–1.2V Native Low-Voltage eFlash: Patented SilvoFlash™ eliminates the ≥1.8V read voltage barrier, allowing whole-chip near-threshold operation with native logic-level readouts; validated across 40nm embedded Flash SoC partnerships and volume DDR5 SPD IC deployments with single-process eFlash/eEEPROM (SilvoFE™) integration.
